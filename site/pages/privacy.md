These pages at tunedecho.com/apps are run by Tuned Echo Systems LLC. Each app has its own privacy policy, linked from its page, that covers what the app itself does with your data.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are read. Google receives information such as the pages you view, your browser and device type, and your approximate location, and sets cookies to tell visits apart. We don't use it for advertising. Google's [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) covers this data.

## Your choice

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, analytics cookies are only set after you accept them in the banner. You can change your choice at any time with **Cookie choices** at the bottom of every page. Anywhere else, you can opt out with Google's [browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) or your browser's cookie settings.

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie, and isn't sent to us.
