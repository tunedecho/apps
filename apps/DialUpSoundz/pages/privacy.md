DialUpSoundz is made by Tuned Echo Systems LLC. In short: your music data stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, we don't have accounts, and there are no analytics or ads.

## What the app uses

**Apple Music.** When you allow it, DialUpSoundz uses Apple Music to search the catalog, play songs, and save playlists to your library. It also reads your library's play counts, skip counts and recently played items so it can record your listening history. These requests go to Apple, and Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers them.

**Your listening history and ratings.** The app stores the plays it records, any play activity file you import, the playlists you make, your Listen Later list, and the ratings, tags and notes you add, in a database on your iPhone. None of it is sent to Tuned Echo Systems or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

**What you copy and paste.** Lists you paste from Claude stay in the app. DialUpSoundz doesn't connect to Claude; you copy text between the two apps yourself. Your chats with Claude are covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

**Your taste summary.** If you choose **Copy prompt with my ratings** or **Taste for Claude**, the app puts a text summary of your ratings on your clipboard: the titles and artists you've rated, with your stars, tags and notes. It goes only where you paste it. Your listening history isn't included.

## What we collect

Nothing. Tuned Echo Systems doesn't run a server for DialUpSoundz and receives no data from the app.

## If this changes

A future version may let you chat with Claude inside the app, using your own Claude API key stored in your iPhone's keychain. If it does, the app will send Claude only what you write and, when you ask, summaries of your listening, never your raw history. This policy will be updated before that version is released.
