DialUpSoundz is made by Tuned Echo Systems LLC. In short: your music data stays on your iPhone. We don't collect it, we don't have accounts, and there are no analytics or ads.

## What the app uses

**Apple Music.** When you allow it, DialUpSoundz uses Apple Music to search the catalog, play songs, and save playlists to your library. It also reads your library's play counts, skip counts and recently played items so it can record your listening history. These requests go to Apple, and Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers them.

**Purchases.** The Data Layer is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. The app asks the App Store whether your Apple Account has bought it. Tuned Echo Systems never sees your payment details, and Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase.

**Your listening history and ratings.** The app stores these in a database on your iPhone:
- the plays it records, and any play activity file you import
- the playlists you make, and your Listen Later list
- the ratings, tags and liner notes you add, including for music that isn't on Apple Music
- the albums and songs you mark "haven't heard"
- the notes and research you keep from Claude

None of it is sent to Tuned Echo Systems or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

**What you copy and paste.** DialUpSoundz doesn't connect to Claude; you copy text between the two apps yourself. Lists and research you paste from Claude stay in the app. The app reads your clipboard only when you tap a paste button. The prompts it copies for you contain none of your data. A research prompt names only the album or song you're asking about. Your chats with Claude are covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.

**Exports.** Your data leaves your iPhone only when you export it and send the file somewhere with the share sheet:
- **Export everything:** a JSON file of all your plays, ratings, Listen Later, playlists and Claude's notes.
- **Export for Claude:** a file for a Claude Project, with your ratings, tags, notes, Listen Later, and counts of what you play most. It never includes individual plays.

Once you upload a file to Claude, Anthropic's privacy policy covers it.

## What we collect

Nothing. Tuned Echo Systems doesn't run a server for DialUpSoundz and receives no data from the app.

## If this changes

A future version may let you chat with Claude inside the app, using your own Claude API key stored in your iPhone's keychain. If it does, the app will send Claude only what you write and, when you ask, summaries of your listening, never your raw history. This policy will be updated before that version is released.
