## Where it's headed

Making playlists from a conversation with a chat service is the part that works well today. The bigger goal is a personal layer over Apple Music: a record of what you listen to and what you think of it, and your own ways to explore it. Your listening history, ratings and tags, research notes from Claude, and recommendations from a Claude Project are already there. The history and charts are early and still growing.

## Free and paid

Making playlists is free. A one-time Data Layer unlock adds rating, recommendations and research with Claude, and your data to browse and export. Your listening is recorded from the day you install the app either way, so nothing is lost by unlocking later.

You need an Apple Music subscription with **Sync Library** turned on to save playlists.
