## Where it's headed

Making playlists from a conversation with a chat service is the part that works well today. The bigger goal is a personal layer over Apple Music: a record of what you listen to and what you think of it, and your own ways to explore it. Your listening history, ratings, tags and an early dashboard are already there.

You need an Apple Music subscription with **Sync Library** turned on to save playlists.
