## Music on the internet, the way it used to feel

The name nods to the dial-up, Napster and Winamp days, when finding music online felt like digging through a friend's record collection. DialUpSoundz brings back the conversation part: you talk with Claude about what you want to hear, and the app turns that conversation into a real Apple Music playlist.

Every song in a playlist is a real Apple Music track that the app found itself, and your listening history never leaves your iPhone. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/) for the details.

You need an Apple Music subscription with **Sync Library** turned on to save playlists.
