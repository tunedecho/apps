## Common questions

### Saving a playlist doesn't work

Saving needs an Apple Music subscription with **Sync Library** turned on, in **Settings › Music**. Also check that DialUpSoundz has access to Apple Music, in the Settings app under DialUpSoundz.

### A song matched the wrong version

Tap it in the draft to see other versions, and play a preview to check. Live, karaoke and cover versions are only preferred when the list asked for them.

### Some songs weren't found

Not everything is on Apple Music in every country. Tap the song and search by hand, or remove it.

### My dashboard is missing older plays

Apple doesn't give apps your full history, so DialUpSoundz starts recording on the day you install it. To fill in earlier years, import Apple's play activity export. The [guide](../guide/#bring-in-your-past-listening) shows how.
