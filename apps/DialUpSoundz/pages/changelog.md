---
title: Changelog
description: What's new in each version of DialUpSoundz.
---

## 0.15.1 · In development

- **Add songs or albums** from a playlist's page: search Apple Music or your library, and tap + to add.

## 0.15.0 · In development

- Playlists can hold **whole albums** as well as songs. Add one from an album's page; it plays as the album.
- See any playlist **by album**: songs are grouped by the album they're from.

## 0.14.1 · In development

- Rating cards have **Preview** and **Play**, to remind you how something sounds, and an option to preview each card as it arrives.
- The first card no longer waits to load.

## 0.14.0 · In development

- The Made tab is now **Playlists**: playlists saved from a list, imported from Apple Music, or built here.
- **Playlist tags:** put a song in a playlist, or take it out, from its page.
- **Import your Apple Music playlists**, choosing which. The originals aren't changed.
- **Save or update** a playlist in Apple Music when you want.
- **Ask the Operator for one like this:** hand it a playlist with your ratings and get a new one in the same spirit.

## 0.13.0 · In development

- Combine tags to filter your ratings, matching all of them or any, and play or save the result.

## 0.12.0 · In development

- **Play a tag** like a playlist, make it into an Apple Music playlist, or save it to Listen Later as a list. Loved and 5 stars work the same way.

## 0.11.0 · In development

- Listen Later has a **Lists** view: your lists, and your playlists, each opening to play it in order or make it into an Apple Music playlist.

## 0.10.0 · In development

- **Save all to Listen Later** from a playlist draft, or swipe a song to save just that one.
- Lists you save from become **lists** in Listen Later.
- Rate what you've heard: **Listened, from Listen Later** in rate mode.

## 0.9.0 · In development

- DialUpSoundz now speaks of your chat app rather than one brand: it works with chat assistants like Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini. Pick yours in **Settings › Your chat app**, and the Open buttons go to it.
- Meet **the Operator**: your chat assistant, set up with your ratings and listening. **Operator setup** replaces the old project setup screen, and **Operator's notes** and the **music profile** replace their old names.

## 0.8.0 · In development

- **Import an export.** Bring a file from Export everything back in, to move to a new iPhone or restore a backup. Nothing already there is duplicated, and your tags come across too.

## 0.7.0 · In development

- Lists from your chat app now say what each song or album is, as well as why it fits. Both are kept with the music when you save a playlist or save albums to Listen Later.
- **Research** on any album or song page. Dig in with your chat assistant, then paste its summary back to keep it with that album: facts, what to listen for, and what to hear next.
- **Data › Operator's notes** gathers everything your assistant has told you, searchable.

## 0.6.0 · In development

- A new **Data** tab replaces the Listening tab. Browse your listening history by artist, album or song, open any of them to play or rate, and see loved and 5-star lists, tags, and the JSON behind it all. Charts live here now.
- Exports, recording and importing Apple's play history moved here from Settings.
- Bring back anything you marked "haven't heard".

## 0.5.0 · In development

- **Recommendations from your chat assistant.** Set up a project in your chat app with your ratings and listening, and ask it for music that fits your taste. It answers with a list the app reads.
- A **music profile** export makes the file for the project: your ratings, tags, Listen Later, and counts of what you play most.
- This replaces copying a summary of your ratings with the prompt.

## 0.4.0 · In development

- **Rate a batch.** Work through your most played albums or songs, your library, an artist's albums in release order, or a search, one at a time.
- Your own default tags, offered every time you rate, and a tag manager to rename, merge and order them.
- Rate music that isn't on Apple Music, too.

## 0.3.0 · In development

- Making playlists is free. A one-time **Data Layer** unlock adds Listen Later, ratings, the dashboard and export. Your listening is recorded from install either way, so nothing is lost by unlocking later.
- **Export my data** saves your plays, ratings, Listen Later and playlists as one JSON file.

## 0.2.0 · In development

- Rate any album or song from 1 to 5 stars, love it or mark it not for me, and add tags and a note.
- A new **Rated** tab lists everything you've rated, filtered by albums or songs, loved, or tag.
- Love what's playing with the heart in the now-playing bar.
- Listen Later items open as full pages: an album shows its songs, and a song links to its album. Mark one listened and the app asks how it was.
- Hand your chat assistant your taste: **Make › Copy prompt with my ratings** copies the prompt together with a summary of your ratings.
- Settings now opens from the gear on the **Listening** tab.

## 0.1.0 · In development

The first version, not yet released.

- Make a playlist from your chat app's list, or a plain list of songs, artists and albums.
- Review matches before saving: swap versions, preview, reorder, remove, and search by hand.
- Album lists, matched to whole albums on Apple Music.
- Play drafts and saved playlists in full, with a now-playing bar and AirPlay.
- Listen Later for songs and albums you want to come back to.
- Listening history recorded on your iPhone, with import of Apple's play activity export.
- A listening dashboard: top artists, songs and albums, plays over time, decades, genres, rediscoveries and more.
