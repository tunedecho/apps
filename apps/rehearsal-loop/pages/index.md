## Apple Music songs and songs you own

Apple doesn't let any app read the audio of Apple Music subscription songs, so Rehearsal Loop asks Apple's player to jump back to the start of your loop instead. That works, with a short gap. Songs you own, such as iTunes purchases, ripped CDs and imported files, play through Rehearsal Loop itself, so it can do much more with them.

| | Apple Music song | Song you own |
|---|---|---|
| Loop a section | Yes, with a short gap | Yes, seamless |
| Slow down or speed up | Usually not; the app tells you | 50 to 150 percent |
| Change pitch | No | Up to 12 semitones up or down |
| Waveform | No | Yes |
| AirPlay | Yes | Yes |
| See the notes and chords (Pro) | Yes, by listening through the microphone | Yes |
| Play along with parts muted (Pro) | No | Yes |

The app shows which kind of song you're playing, and tells you when a control isn't available for it.

## Free and Pro

Looping is free, for good. Rehearsal Loop Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, for when you want help learning the part by ear.

| Free | Pro |
|---|---|
| Loop any section with one tap | Several named loops per song, each with its own tempo |
| Tempo and pitch for songs you own | Key, chords and notes of the loop as sheet music, tab or a piano roll |
| The loop editor and waveform | Notes written for your instrument, including B♭, E♭ and F instruments |
| Your loop and tempo remembered for every song | Edit notes, play them back, and share them as MusicXML or MIDI |
| Import your own audio files | Transcribe Apple Music loops through the microphone |
| AirPlay, headphone and lock-screen buttons | Play along with any part muted or soloed |

Pro works on all your devices with the same Apple Account, and with your Family Sharing group.

Transcription and play along are new, and still being tested on iPhone before release.
