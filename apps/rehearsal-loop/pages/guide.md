## Pick a song

Tap **Songs**.
- **Use what's playing in the Music app** picks up the song you're already listening to.
- **Search** looks through Apple Music and your library.
- With nothing typed, you can browse **Your files**, your **Playlists**, your **owned songs**, and the **Apple Music songs in your library**.

The first time, allow Rehearsal Loop to use Apple Music.

## Add your own songs

Songs you add yourself get everything: a seamless loop, tempo, pitch, and play along with Pro. In **Songs**, under **Your files**:
- Tap **Import audio files…** and pick one or more from the Files app.
- Or share or AirDrop an audio file to Rehearsal Loop from another app. It opens and starts playing.
- Or, on a Mac, open your iPhone in Finder, go to the **Files** tab and drag files onto Rehearsal Loop.

Your files also appear in the Files app, under **On My iPhone**. Swipe left on one in **Songs** to delete it.

## Set a loop

1. Tap the big **Loop** button where the section starts. That sets **A**.
2. Tap again where it ends. That sets **B**, and the loop starts.
3. Tap again to turn the loop off. A and B are kept.
4. Tap again to turn it back on.

**Restart** jumps back to A. **Clear** forgets both points so you can start over. Your loop and tempo are saved for each song and come back the next time you open it.

## Fine-tune the start and end

Tap the **A** or **B** card under the button to open the loop editor.
- Drag to move the point under the fixed centre line, and pinch to zoom.
- Use the nudge buttons to move it by small steps. Choose the coarse step in **Settings**.
- Use the listen buttons to hear just before or just after the point.

Songs you own show a waveform. Apple Music songs show a ruler instead, because the app can't read their audio.

## Save several loops per song (Pro)

Learning a whole song usually means working on a few sections. Once a loop is set, tap **Save this loop** under the A and B cards and give it a name. Each saved loop keeps its own A, B and tempo.
- Tap a saved loop to jump to it. It starts from A at its own tempo.
- While a saved loop is selected, moving its points or changing the tempo updates it.
- Touch and hold a saved loop to rename or delete it.
- **Clear** stops using the saved loop without deleting it.

The song remembers which loop you were on, so it comes back next time.

## Slow it down

For songs you own, the **Tempo** slider goes from 50 to 150 percent without changing the pitch.

To change the key as well, turn on **Show pitch control** in **Settings**, then shift by up to 12 semitones either way.

For Apple Music songs, Rehearsal Loop tries to change the speed, but Apple's player usually ignores it. When that happens, the slider is turned off and the app says why.

## Practice over AirPlay

Tap the **AirPlay** button to send the sound to a speaker or TV. AirPlay audio arrives about two seconds after the app plays it, so Rehearsal Loop takes that delay off your taps for songs you own. A and B land where you heard them, not where the player was.

## Hands-free buttons

Your headphone and lock-screen controls work while you play:

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| Play/pause | Play or pause |
| Next | Same as tapping the Loop button |
| Previous | Back to A |

## See the notes and chords (Pro)

Set a loop, then tap **Transcribe loop**. Rehearsal Loop works out the key, the chords and the notes of the loop. It all happens on your iPhone.

1. **Bars in loop** and **Beats per bar.** The start of the loop counts as beat 1. The app guesses how many bars the loop is from the rhythm. If the tempo it shows looks wrong, change the bar count.
2. **Parts to transcribe.** Pick the full mix, or one or more parts: vocals, bass, guitar, piano, drums and other. For example, guitar and piano together for the chords, or vocals for the melody. Tap **Listen** to hear the mix you've chosen, then tap **Transcribe**. Splitting the parts takes a few seconds, once per loop.
3. **Instrument.** Notes are shown at concert pitch, or written for your instrument: guitar or bass with tab, or trumpet, clarinet, saxophones and horn in B♭, E♭ or F.
4. **View.** Switch between **Sheet music** and a piano roll. The note that's sounding is highlighted as the song or the notes play.

Treat the result as a first draft. Play the notes back to check them by ear, and tap a note in the piano roll to fix its pitch, move it, change its length or delete it. Your edits are saved.

Tap **Share** to send the transcription as **MusicXML**, for notation apps such as MuseScore, or as **MIDI**.

## See the notes of an Apple Music song (Pro)

Apps can't read the sound of Apple Music songs, so Rehearsal Loop listens instead.

1. Play the song through the iPhone's speaker or a speaker in the room. Headphones won't work, because the microphone can't hear them.
2. Open **Transcribe loop** and tap **Record one pass**. The first time, allow the microphone.
3. Keep the room quiet while one pass of the loop is recorded. Then it's transcribed like any other loop.

The louder and clearer the playback, the better the notes. A speaker in front of the phone works better than the phone's own speaker. **Record again** replaces the recording. The recording stays on your iPhone.

## Play along with the band (Pro)

For songs you own and files you've added, tap **Play along**. Rehearsal Loop splits the loop into vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and other, then loops them together at your tempo.
- **M** mutes a part and **S** solos it, and each part has its own volume.
- **Practice without…** mutes one part, so you can play it yourself.
- **Hear only…** solos one part, so you can hear exactly what it plays.

Some sound bleeds between the parts. Vocals, drums and bass come out cleanest; guitar and piano less so. Play along doesn't work with Apple Music songs, because it needs the song's audio.

## Rehearsal Loop Pro

Looping, tempo, pitch, the loop editor and your own files are free. Pro is a one-time purchase that adds saved loops, transcription and play along. Tap anything marked **PRO**, or go to **Settings**, then tap **Unlock Pro**.

Pro works on every device signed in to the same Apple Account, and for your Family Sharing group. On a new device, tap **Restore Purchases** in **Settings**.

## Get tighter loops on Apple Music songs

Apple Music songs loop by jumping back, which leaves a short gap. The gap is smaller when the song is downloaded. If the app says a song is streaming, download it in the Music app and open it again.
