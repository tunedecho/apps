## Pick a song

Tap **Songs**.
- **Use what's playing in the Music app** picks up the song you're already listening to.
- **Search** looks through Apple Music and your library.
- With nothing typed, you can browse your **Playlists**, your **owned songs**, and the **Apple Music songs in your library**.

The first time, allow Rehearsal Loop to use Apple Music.

## Set a loop

1. Tap the big **Loop** button where the section starts. That sets **A**.
2. Tap again where it ends. That sets **B**, and the loop starts.
3. Tap again to turn the loop off. A and B are kept.
4. Tap again to turn it back on.

**Restart** jumps back to A. **Clear** forgets both points so you can start over. Your loop and tempo are saved for each song and come back the next time you open it.

## Fine-tune the start and end

Tap the **A** or **B** card under the button to open the loop editor.
- Drag to move the point under the fixed centre line, and pinch to zoom.
- Use the nudge buttons to move it by small steps. Choose the coarse step in **Settings**.
- Use the listen buttons to hear just before or just after the point.

Songs you own show a waveform. Apple Music songs show a ruler instead, because the app can't read their audio.

## Slow it down

For songs you own, the **Tempo** slider goes from 50 to 150 percent without changing the pitch.

To change the key as well, turn on **Show pitch control** in **Settings**, then shift by up to 12 semitones either way.

For Apple Music songs, Rehearsal Loop tries to change the speed, but Apple's player usually ignores it. When that happens, the slider is turned off and the app says why.

## Practice over AirPlay

Tap the **AirPlay** button to send the sound to a speaker or TV. AirPlay audio arrives about two seconds after the app plays it, so Rehearsal Loop takes that delay off your taps for songs you own. A and B land where you heard them, not where the player was.

## Hands-free buttons

Your headphone and lock-screen controls work while you play:

| Button | Does |
|---|---|
| Play/pause | Play or pause |
| Next | Same as tapping the Loop button |
| Previous | Back to A |

## Get tighter loops on Apple Music songs

Apple Music songs loop by jumping back, which leaves a short gap. The gap is smaller when the song is downloaded. If the app says a song is streaming, download it in the Music app and open it again.
