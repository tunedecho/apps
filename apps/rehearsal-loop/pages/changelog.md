## 0.1.0 · In development

The first version, not yet released.

- Loop any section with one big button: tap for A, tap for B, tap to turn the loop off and on.
- Apple Music songs loop at normal speed. The loop tightens itself by measuring how long each jump back takes.
- Songs you own loop seamlessly, with tempo from 50 to 150 percent and optional pitch shift.
- Loop editor with a zoomable waveform for songs you own, fine nudges, and listen buttons for each point.
- Song picker with what's playing in the Music app, search, playlists, owned songs, and Apple Music songs in your library.
- AirPlay, with tap timing corrected for AirPlay's delay.
- Headphone and lock-screen buttons control the loop.
- Loops and tempo saved for every song.
- A new app icon.

**Built, still being tested.** These are in the app but haven't been tried on an iPhone with real songs yet, so they may change before release:
- Import your own audio files from the Files app, the share sheet, AirDrop or a Mac.
- Keep several named practice loops for each song, each with its own tempo.
- Transcribe the loop: key, chords and notes, shown as sheet music, tab or a piano roll, with note editing. Share it as MusicXML or MIDI.
- Split the loop into vocals, drums, bass, guitar, piano and other on the iPhone, then play along with any part muted or on its own.
- Transcribe Apple Music loops by recording one pass through the microphone while the song plays aloud.
- Rehearsal Loop Pro, a one-time purchase that unlocks transcription, play along and saved practice loops. Looping, tempo, pitch, the loop editor and your own files stay free.
