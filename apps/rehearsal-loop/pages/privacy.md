Rehearsal Loop is made by Tuned Echo Systems LLC. In short: everything the app works with stays on your iPhone or iPad. We don't collect it, there are no accounts, and the app has no analytics or ads.

## What the app uses

**Apple Music and your music library.** When you allow it, Rehearsal Loop uses Apple Music to search the catalog and play songs, and reads your music library to list your songs and playlists. Apple Music requests go to Apple, and Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) covers them.

**The microphone.** Apple doesn't let any app read the sound of Apple Music songs. To transcribe an Apple Music loop, the app can listen to it through the microphone while it plays aloud. It uses the microphone only after you allow it, and only while you're recording one pass of a loop. The recording is saved on your device, analysed there, and replaced when you record that loop again. It's never uploaded.

**Your own audio files.** Files you import, share or AirDrop to Rehearsal Loop are copied into the app's folder on your device, which you can also see in the Files app. They stay there until you delete them.

**Working out the music.** Separating a loop into parts and transcribing its chords and notes happen entirely on your device. There's no server.

**What the app saves.** Your loops, tempo and pitch settings, practice loops and transcriptions, with any notes you've edited, are stored on your device. Like other app data, they can be included in your iCloud or computer backup. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Sharing.** A transcription leaves your device only when you tap Share and send it somewhere, as a MusicXML or MIDI file. Wherever you send it, that app's or service's privacy policy covers it.

**Purchases.** Rehearsal Loop Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled by Apple. The app asks the App Store whether your Apple Account has bought it. Tuned Echo Systems never sees your payment details, and Apple's privacy policy covers the purchase.

## What we collect

Nothing. Tuned Echo Systems doesn't run a server for Rehearsal Loop and receives no data from the app. If you email us for support, we see your email address and what you send, and use it only to reply.

## If this changes

A future version may support a Bluetooth MIDI foot pedal. If it does, the app will connect only to the pedal you pair and read only its button presses. This policy will be updated before any version that changes what the app uses or sends is released.
